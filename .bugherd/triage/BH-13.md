## Description
Need to have a Post-Operative or After Care tab for all procedures so patients can refer to and review before calling the office.

## Screenshot Analysis
Patient Resources page. Pin on the resource card grid — New Patients, Patient Forms, Insurance & Payment, FAQ (plus Reviews and Promotions below the fold). No After Care card present.

## Page Analysis
The aftercare content **already exists** — 8 pages under `src/pages/aftercare/`: botox-injections, bruise-management, cleanings-checkups, crowns-bridges, dermal-filler-injections, root-canals, routine-fillings, veneers.

They are effectively unreachable:
- `src/components/Footer.astro:22` links only to `/aftercare/cleanings-checkups/`
- Three Masterpiece Aesthetics service pages deep-link to their own aftercare page
- There is **no `/aftercare/` index page** and no entry point from `/patients/`

## Assessment
Actionable. This is a navigation/IA gap, not a content gap. No client input needed.

## Plan
1. Create `src/pages/aftercare/index.astro` — a hub listing all 8 aftercare pages, grouped (General & Family / Masterpiece Aesthetics), matching the `/patients/` card-grid pattern.
2. Add an "After Care Instructions" card to the `resources` array in `src/pages/patients/index.astro` (after "Patient Forms"), href `/aftercare/`, with an appropriate icon.
3. Repoint `src/components/Footer.astro:22` from `/aftercare/cleanings-checkups/` to `/aftercare/`.
4. Add a "Post-Operative Care" link on each service page that has a matching aftercare page (currently only the three aesthetics pages do).
5. Trailing slashes on all new links per CLAUDE.md.
